# GroundUp AI: Vocabulary and Conversion Style Dictionary

This document defines the mandatory language, conversion vocabulary, and communication standards for GroundUp AI. It prevents overly robotic, hyper-technical, academic, or gamified copy from creeping into user-facing marketing, landing screens, product guides, and documentation.

---

## 1. Core Conversion Principles

1. **Humanized, High-Conviction Real Estate Authority:**
   Our audience consists of real estate developers, property owners, finance controllers, general contractors, and commercial lending officers. They evaluate tools within seconds based on **money, time, risk, and clarity**.
2. **Simplified on the Surface, Precise Underneath:**
   Avoid presenting low-level software plumbing (cryptography algorithms, internal micro-services) as product benefits. Translate software features directly into financial and operational outcomes:
   - Preserved contingency reserves
   - Same-day bank draw approval
   - Elimination of surprise job losses
   - Protection against subcontractor title liens
3. **Institutional and Professional (Zero Arcade Gamification):**
   GroundUp manages millions of dollars in construction loans. Strictly prohibit gaming mechanics, hidden easter eggs, "secret unlocks", or celebratory badges that undermine institutional trust.

## 3. The Core Financial Value Pillars

Every landing page section, widget, and advisor explanation must reinforce these tangible financial pillars:

1. **Live Timeline & Budget (Real-Time P&L):**
   - Instant visibility into Net Margin, Gross Profit, and Project Budget Variance (Under Budget / Over Budget).
   - Eliminates post-close financial surprises.
2. **Inspection Delay & Carrying Cost Burn:**
   - When a municipal inspection is postponed or flagged, calculate the real daily debt carry:
     $$\text{Daily Interest Carry} = \text{Drawn Loan Principal} \times \left(\frac{\text{APR}}{365}\right)$$
   - Show how cost code isolation allows unaffected trades to draw funds and prevent compounding carrying losses.
3. **Same-Day Bank Draw Assembly & Bank Email Reconciliation:**
   - Subcontractor invoices + municipal inspection cards + statutory lien waivers + 10% retainage withholdings compiled into bank-ready AIA G702/G703 packages.
   - Bank email notifications and wire confirmations reconciled directly against budget line items.
4. **Statutory Lien and Retainage Governance:**
   - 10% AIA A201 retainage withheld automatically:
     $$\text{Net Payable} = \text{Gross Incurred} - \text{Retainage Withholding}$$
   - Statutory progress releases (CA Civil Code Sections 8132, 8134, 8136, 8138) verified before wire release to safeguard title.

## 4. Mandatory Copy Guardrails

- **Zero Em Dashes:** Strictly prohibit unicode em dashes (`\u2014`), HTML `&mdash;`, or double hyphens `--` as punctuation. Use colons, commas, parentheses, or clean sentence restructuring.
- **Zero CEO / CTO References:** Use professional corporate titles: **Developer / Owner**, **Finance Controller**, **Super Admin**, or **Systems Architect**.
- **Visuals Over Multi-Click Interactive Work:** High-traffic visitors do not want to click through 6-step interactive forms. Show rich visual dashboard previews, Gantt timelines, reconciliation sheets, and clear summary graphs upfront.
